# Quality gate

Approve only when all essential checks pass.

## Scene

- The source relationship remains understandable.
- One anchor clearly dominates.
- No unrelated landmark, person, product, or symbol has been invented.
- Fidelity mode is stated honestly.

## Composition

- The eye has an entry, focal encounter, movement, and quiet exit.
- Paper is active space, not unused background.
- Photography and drawing are unequal but balanced.
- The poster reads at thumbnail size.

## Translation

- Repeated detail is compressed, not traced.
- One drawing grammar dominates.
- The drawing continues a real source direction.
- The result is calmer than the source photograph.

## Color

- Exactly one added saturated hue appears in an individual poster.
- The hue comes from a visible source cue.
- It touches or crosses the photographic anchor.
- Removing it would weaken balance or movement.

## Material

- The main boundary has an irregular fibrous tear.
- No sticker outline, heavy shadow, curled corner, or fake 3D paper appears.
- Grain, ink loss, and scan texture remain subordinate.

## Text

- Text is absent or limited to one useful short line.
- Supplied wording is exact and legible.
- Text remains smaller than the image and structural color.
- No random metadata or fabricated brand language appears.

## Series

- Paper tone, drawing family, text scale, and edge behavior match.
- Each poster has a distinct source relationship and structural hue.
- The cover uses unequal fragments and does not become a thumbnail grid.
